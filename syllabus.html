Syllabus

Three weeks, one module per week. Each week lists what you should be able to do by the end of it and the work you are expected to complete.

Week 1

How LLMs Generate Answers

Learning objectives

  • Be able to explain where the term Artificial Intelligence came from and how LLMs generate answers for you.
  • Be able to explain how your words are turned into tokens and how LLMs predict the next token to give you the most accurate answer.
  • Learn how LLMs are becoming more useful and why their predictions aren’t always correct.

Assignments

  • Read Module 1.
  • Complete Quiz 1.
Week 2

Recognizing Common Mistakes

Learning objectives

  • Recognize the common mistakes that people make while using AI.
  • Describe examples of AI providing misleading advice.
  • Learn how to double check the output of AI against a reputable source that you trust.

Assignments

  • Read Module 2.
  • Complete Quiz 2.
Week 3

Realistic Concerns vs. Myths

Learning objectives

  • Recognize the extreme positions that people may take on AI.
  • Learn about the common myths people have surrounding AI.
  • Be able to separate realistic concerns about AI from the myths.

Assignments

  • Read Module 3.
  • Complete Quiz 3.
